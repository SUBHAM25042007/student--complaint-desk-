CampusCare - Student Complaint & Anti-Ragging Portal
====================================================
Pages (5): index.html (Home), complaint.html (File Complaint), track.html (Complaint Tracking),
           support.html (Student Support & Safety Guidance), admin.html (Admin Dashboard)
Shared:    style.css (design, dark mode, responsive), script.js (validation, storage, tracking, admin)

Run: open index.html in a browser. No server needed.
Admin password: admin123 (change ADMIN_PASSWORD at the top of script.js).
Data is stored in the browser (localStorage), so complaints are visible on the same browser only.
Add your college's anti-ragging committee contact in support.html.
