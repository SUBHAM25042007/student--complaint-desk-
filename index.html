What can you report?

🚫

Ragging

Physical or mental harassment of juniors. It is a criminal offence.

🚨

Bullying

Teasing, threats, social exclusion or cyberbullying.

⚠️

Harassment

Any unwanted behaviour that makes you feel unsafe.

🛡️

Campus Safety

Unsafe areas, poor lighting, security problems.

🏠

Hostel Facilities

Food, water, cleanliness, rooms and wardens.

📖

Academic Concerns

Attendance, marks, faculty or exam issues.

How it works

File

Fill the short form. You can hide your name from the admin.

Get your ID

You receive a tracking ID like CC-123456. Keep it safe.

Review

The admin reviews it, urgent cases first.

Track

See the status and the admin remark any time.

In danger right now? Call 112. For ragging, the 24x7 National Anti-Ragging Helpline is 1800-180-5522. See the Support & Safety page.
