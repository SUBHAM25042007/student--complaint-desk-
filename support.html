Guidance

What counts as ragging?

Any conduct that causes or may cause physical or psychological harm or fear to a student, such as forced actions, abuse, humiliation, money demands or forced tasks.

What should I do if it happens to me?

Move to a safe place, tell a trusted friend, warden or faculty, write down what happened (who, when, where), and file a complaint here. If you are in danger, call 112.

What if I see someone else being harassed?

Do not confront the group alone. Help the person move away safely and report it. You can hide your name while reporting.

Hostel and campus safety tips

Avoid isolated places at night, share your location with a friend, keep important numbers saved, and report poor lighting or broken locks quickly.

Cyberbullying

Do not reply. Take screenshots, block the account, and report it with the date and platform name.

I am feeling very stressed or low

Talk to someone you trust. Free mental health support is available through Tele-MANAS on 14416.

Note: this is a student project. For real incidents, always also contact your college authorities.
